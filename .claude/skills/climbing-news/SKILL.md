# Climbing news → Instagram story pitches

Goal: produce a morning briefing of **5–8 candidate stories** that a person can
quickly pick from and turn into Instagram posts. You pitch; a human decides and posts.
Never post to Instagram or any public channel yourself.

## 1. Gather (last 24–36 hours, unless told otherwise)

Use web search and page fetching. Cover a spread of these, plus anything they link to:

| Area | Sources |
|---|---|
| General / rock | climbing.com, ukclimbing.com/news, gripped.com, planetmountain.com, eveningsends.com, 8a.nu (news) |
| Alpine / big mountains | alpinist.com, explorersweb.com, planetmountain.com (alpinism) |
| Competition | ifsc-climbing.org (results & news), national federation sites, Olympic climbing coverage |
| Gear / industry / access | Access Fund, BMC / British Climbing, brand press releases (only if genuinely newsworthy) |

Also run a few open searches such as: `climbing first ascent this week`,
`bouldering send news`, `IFSC results`, `climbing news today`, `mountaineering expedition news`.

Things that make good stories: first ascents and hard repeats (9a+/V15+ and notable
flashes/onsights), first female/youngest/fastest ascents, comp results and upsets,
expedition summits or rescues, access/crag closures, records, big film or book
releases, notable gear recalls, human-interest pieces.

## 2. Verify and filter

- Confirm each story with **at least two independent sources**, or one primary source
  (the climber's own statement, the federation's official results). Mark anything less
  as **UNCONFIRMED**.
- Note the publish date of every source; drop anything older than the window unless
  it is a genuinely new development.
- Merge duplicates (the same send covered by several outlets is one story).
- Skip rumours about people's private lives, and be careful and respectful with
  accidents and deaths — flag those as **SENSITIVE** and suggest a tone.
- Don't invent grades, quotes, or details. If a grade is proposed/unconfirmed, say so.

## 3. Rank

Score each story 1–5 on: newsworthiness, broad appeal to the audience, visual potential,
and timeliness. Order by total. Include at least one "quick win" (easy single-image post)
and, if available, one longer-form/carousel idea.

## 4. Output format

Start with a one-line summary ("7 stories, top pick: …"), then for each story:

```
### <n>. <Punchy headline>  — score X/20  [CONFIRMED | UNCONFIRMED] [SENSITIVE?]
**What happened:** 2–3 factual sentences (who, what, where, grade, when).
**Why it matters / hook:** the angle for the post.
**Suggested format:** single image | carousel (outline slides) | reel | story poll
**Draft caption:** ≤ 2,200 chars, first line is the hook, credit the climber's
  handle if known, end with a question to drive comments.
**Hashtags:** 8–15, mix of broad (#climbing #bouldering) and specific.
**Visuals:** what image/video would work and who owns it (photographer/athlete
  handle). Note: ask permission / credit — never assume photos are free to reuse.
**Sources:** bullet list of links with publish dates.
```

End with a short "Also noted (not pitched)" list of minor items, and "Coming up"
(comps or events in the next 7 days worth preparing for).

## 5. Delivery

- If a Gmail tool is available and the request (or scheduled task) asks for email,
  send the briefing to the account owner only, subject
  `Climbing news pitches — <date>`. Send nowhere else.
- Otherwise, return the briefing as the final message.
